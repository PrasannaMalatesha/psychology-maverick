MindMarket · Psychology Maverick

Warm storybook, grounded receipts.

One system for every surface. A cream-paper canvas, Bricolage Grotesque for the storybook voice, Inter for reading, and JetBrains Mono for the citation “receipts.” Everything below is sourced live from design-system/tokens.css and design-system/icons.js.

Color

One structural accent (grass), one action accent (coral), a warm ink for text and borders. Bright accents stay decorative — never repurposed as strict success/error states, with the single exception of coral for the crisis edge and a dedicated danger red for destructive text.

Typography

Three families, one rhythm. Display type carries authority through scale and tight tracking; mono is reserved for anything that reads as a receipt — citation numbers, source codes, taxonomic labels.

Iconography

One coherent stroke set on a 24×24 grid, 1.8 stroke, round caps and joins. The previously hand-drawn heart and settings icons were off-grid and lopsided — they’re highlighted below, now redrawn to correct, symmetric geometry.

Easing cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) for entrances, (.2,0,0,1) for emphasis. Durations 130 / 220 / 340 ms. All motion collapses to 0 under prefers-reduced-motion.
