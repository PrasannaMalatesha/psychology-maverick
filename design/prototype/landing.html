What is the difference between classical and operant conditioning?

Classical conditioning links two stimuli, so a neutral cue comes to trigger a response.1 Operant conditioning links a behavior to its consequence, making it more or less likely to recur.2

Built to be safe, not only helpful.

Some people arrive in a hard moment. Psychology Maverick explains what authoritative sources say, and never diagnoses or prescribes. If a question signals real distress, it does not reach for the library. It points to people who can help, right away.
